How this works

Tap any car — colors show status at a glance: green = free, red = out, gray = off duty.

Take a car — enter your staff number, confirm the quick vehicle check, and go.

Return a car — tap the loaned car and confirm the staff number returning it.

Remarks — flag an issue on any car any time, no login needed (up to 5 open remarks per car). A small amber dot shows on cars with an open remark.

Shift & team — the bar under the header shows which of the 4 rotating teams (A/B/C/D) is on duty and whether it's Day (06:00–18:00) or Night (18:00–06:00), calculated automatically.
